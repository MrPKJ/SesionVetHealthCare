About Sesion Vet Health Care

Three decades of passion for animal health and well-being

Welcome

Welcome to Sesion Vet Health Care

At Sesion Vet Health Care, we are driven by one mission — to advance animal wellness through innovation, quality and trust. As an emerging veterinary healthcare company from India, we are committed to delivering modern healthcare and nutritional solutions that support healthier livestock and stronger farm productivity.


Our journey began with a vision to bridge the gap between science and practical animal care. By combining technical expertise, industry knowledge and customer-focused service, we provide dependable veterinary products designed to meet the evolving needs of farmers, veterinarians and the livestock industry.

What We Do

Our Core Activities

We supply, trade and distribute a comprehensive range of animal health products across India and globally.

Veterinary Medicines

Injections, bolus, syrups, and liquid formulations for cattle and poultry health management.

Feed Supplements

Mineral mixtures, vitamin supplements, and chelated minerals for improved livestock productivity.

Poultry Healthcare

Complete range of poultry feed additives, health products, and water sanitizers.

Export & Supply

Nationwide distribution and export services bringing quality products to every corner.

Products

Our Product Line

Our product portfolio covers every aspect of animal healthcare — from therapeutic medicines to preventive supplements. Each product is carefully sourced from certified manufacturers and tested for purity, potency and safety before distribution.


Key categories include Injections, Bolus, Syrups, Liquid Feed Supplements, Mineral Mixtures, Powders, and Chelated Minerals & Vitamins for both cattle and poultry.

Quality Control

Uncompromising Quality

At Sesion Vet Health Care, quality is not just a standard — it is the foundation of everything we do. We are committed to delivering reliable veterinary and animal healthcare solutions that meet the highest levels of safety, consistency and performance.


Every product undergoes a carefully monitored quality assurance process, including raw material inspection, formulation monitoring, in-process evaluation and final product testing. Our focus is to ensure that every solution reaching farmers and veterinarians delivers dependable results in real-world conditions.

Our Vision

To become a strong and globally recognized player in the veterinary industry, bringing world-class animal healthcare solutions to every livestock owner across India and beyond.

Our Mission

To serve the livestock sector with scientific, technical and clinical solutions that enhance animal productivity, reduce disease burden, and improve the livelihoods of farmers.
