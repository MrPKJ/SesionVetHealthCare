Who We Are

Our Aim is Simple – Better Health, Better Productivity

We are proud to introduce SESION VET HEALTH CARE, a progressive and innovation-driven animal healthcare company from India, dedicated to strengthening the future of the global veterinary industry. With a strong commitment to animal wellness, we provide scientific, nutritional and technical solutions designed to improve livestock health, productivity and overall performance. At SESION VET HEALTH CARE, we specialize in the manufacturing, supply and distribution of high-quality veterinary medicines, feed supplements and animal nutrition products for cattle, poultry and other livestock.


Our wide product portfolio includes injections, bolus, syrups, liquid feed supplements, mineral mixtures, chelated minerals, vitamin supplements and nutritional formulations developed to support animal growth, immunity and performance. Every product is selected and developed with a focus on safety, consistency and field effectiveness.


We are committed to delivering trusted healthcare products, poultry healthcare solutions, feed additives, nutritional supplements, water sanitizers and advanced veterinary formulations that help farmers and businesses achieve better productivity and healthier livestock. At SESION VET HEALTH CARE, we believe that quality animal care is the foundation of a stronger agricultural future. Through innovation, reliability and customer-focused service, we aim to become a trusted partner for veterinarians, distributors, farmers and the livestock industry worldwide.
